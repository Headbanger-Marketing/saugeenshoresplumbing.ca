FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Are you licensed plumbers in Bruce County?

Licensed and insured, and any permitted job in Saugeen Shores is filed with the municipality and inspected. Renovation work that will be reviewed later should start with paperwork in order.

Do you answer emergency calls in Port Elgin and Southampton?

Around the clock, all year. Lake-effect storms snap lines and knock out power at bad hours, and a flooding basement in January does not wait for morning, cottage or not.

Can you check our cottage plumbing at spring opening?

Yes, and we book opening-week visits early. Freeze damage, failed tanks, and split lines that sat empty all winter are the standard finds, and catching them in week one keeps your summer on schedule.

We are converting our cottage to year-round. Can you handle the plumbing?

That is a specialty of ours. Seasonal-to-permanent conversions need a full review of supply, drains, and freeze protection, and we plan the work so the cottage upgrades without losing its summer character.

Is our cottage well water safe to treat without a lab result?

We are plumbers, not a lab, so bring the results from your public health sample and we size filtration or UV to exactly what is in the water. Guessing at treatment just wastes cottage money.

Does lakeside living really need a sump pump?

Near the shore the water table is high and new homes are literally built on it. A properly sized pump with battery backup is inexpensive protection against the storms that roll straight in off Lake Huron.

How does your pricing work?

Written quotes before work begins, and seasonal jobs get scheduled around your opening and closing dates rather than our convenience. No surprise line items, ever.

Where do you work around Saugeen Shores?

Port Elgin, Southampton, and Inverhuron, plus Northport, Tara, Paisley, and the cottage roads between. If you can see the lake or hear it on a still night, you are probably in our range.

Do you serve areas outside of Saugeen Shores, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Saugeen Shores and nearby communities including Port Elgin, Southampton, Inverhuron, Northport, and the surrounding area.
